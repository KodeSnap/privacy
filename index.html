KodeSnap Privacy Policy

App: KodeSnap
Package name: com.kodesnap.scanner
Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how chanwingchow ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the KodeSnap mobile application ("KodeSnap" or "the App"). KodeSnap is a barcode/QR scanner, text recognition (OCR), and document scanning app.

Summary: KodeSnap is designed to process scan content primarily on your device. You do not need to register or sign in to use the App, and we do not automatically upload your scan results to our own servers. If you choose to send feedback in Settings, your message, optional contact details, and limited technical information are sent to us through Firebase as described below. The App uses third-party advertising, analytics, crash reporting, consent-management, and Google Play services that may collect or receive the technical and usage information described below. When you scan a supported retail product barcode, the App also requests product information from Open Food Facts as described in Section 5.

1. Information We Process

Depending on how you use the App, the following information may be processed and stored on your device:

  • Scan and recognition content, such as barcode/QR code values, recognized text, and generated PDF documents.
  • Scan history metadata, including scan type, timestamp, and favorite status.
  • Camera and selected images, when you scan with the camera or choose an image from your photo library for recognition.
  • App preferences, such as your selected display language.
  • App activity and diagnostics, such as app launches, screens and features used, scan type and source (but not the scanned content), session information, crash logs, performance and error information.
  • Device, network, and advertising information, such as device model, operating system and App version, language, approximate location derived from IP address, IP address, advertising ID or other app/device identifiers, and ad impressions, clicks, and other advertising interactions.
  • Privacy choices, including consent status and advertising privacy preferences managed through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).
  • Optional feedback: Only when you submit the feedback form, we receive your message, any contact details you enter, App version, Android version, device manufacturer/model, App language, and submission time. Firebase Authentication creates or reuses a pseudonymous anonymous user ID to authorize the submission and limit abuse. Scan results, images, documents, and logs are not automatically attached to feedback.

Scan results may contain sensitive information depending on what you scan—for example, URLs, contact details, Wi‑Fi credentials, calendar events, or other data encoded in a barcode or document. This information is created by your use of the App and is stored locally unless you delete it or uninstall the App.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Provide core App features, including scanning, text recognition, and document scanning;
  • Save and display your scan history;
  • Let you preview, copy, share, or open scan results when you choose to do so;
  • Retrieve food product, nutrition, and ingredient information for supported retail product barcodes;
  • Remember your in-app settings, such as language preference;
  • Receive and respond to feedback, investigate reported problems, and prevent feedback abuse;
  • Measure App usage, diagnose crashes and errors, and improve reliability;
  • Request, display, measure, secure, and prevent fraud relating to advertisements;
  • Comply with consent, privacy, legal, and regulatory obligations.

We do not send your scan content to advertising or analytics providers, and we do not sell your scan content. Google and its advertising partners may process device, network, identifier, consent, and App interaction information to provide and measure ads as described in Section 5.

3. Where Data Is Stored

Your scan history is stored locally in an on-device database. Document scans are saved as PDF files in the App's private app storage. We do not automatically transmit your scan content to our own servers. If you include scan content in a feedback message, that text is sent as part of your message.

Important — device backup: If you have Android device backup enabled (for example, through your Google account), your App data—including scan history and documents—may be uploaded to Google's cloud servers as part of your device backup. This constitutes a transfer of your data to Google, whose data practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. If you do not want App data included in backup, please review your device backup settings.

You can delete individual history entries inside the App. Deleting a document entry also removes its local PDF file.

If you uninstall the App, locally stored App data is generally removed from your device, subject to your device and operating system behavior.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: Used to scan barcodes/QR codes and capture images for text recognition. If you deny camera access, you may still use the photo picker to select images for scanning where supported.
  • Photos / media picker: Used only when you choose an image from your library. The App accesses only the item(s) you select.

5. Third-Party Services

KodeSnap uses Google on-device and Play services technologies to provide recognition features, including:

  • Google ML Kit for barcode scanning and text recognition;
  • Google Play services ML Kit Document Scanner for document scanning and PDF generation.

Processing for these features generally occurs on your device or through Google-provided components on your device. Google's handling of data may be governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Open Food Facts

After scanning a valid EAN or UPC product barcode, KodeSnap sends that product code, the App language, and the App name/version to Open Food Facts to retrieve product names, nutrition facts, ingredients, and related information. This lookup does not send your other scan history, QR code contents, contacts, Wi-Fi contents, images, or documents. As part of the network request, the service receives your IP address.

Product results are temporarily cached on your device, kept separate from the scan history database, and excluded from Android system backup. The App limits requests and respects server rate limits. Open Food Facts information is supplied by its community and may be incomplete or inaccurate; verify the packaging, especially for allergens. For the service's practices, see Open Food Facts Privacy Policy.

Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics

KodeSnap uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, services provided by Google LLC (1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA), to measure usage, diagnose crashes and errors, and improve the App. This information is generally pseudonymous rather than anonymous. These services may collect the following data categories:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version);
  • App instance ID (a pseudonymous identifier unique to each App installation);
  • App usage data (feature usage frequency, session duration, screens viewed);
  • App version and first-launch timestamp;
  • Crash logs, stack traces, diagnostic state, and information related to crashes and errors.

Our custom analytics events report categories such as scan mode, scan source, recognition language, success or failure reason, result type, and result action. They do not include the barcode value, recognized text, document image, or generated PDF content. We use this data to understand App usage, improve features, and diagnose reliability issues; we do not use scan content for advertising.

6. Sensitive Personal Information

When you scan certain types of barcodes, QR codes, or documents, the scanned content may contain sensitive personal information under applicable data protection laws, including but not limited to:

  • Personal contact details (phone numbers, email addresses embedded in QR codes);
  • Account credentials (Wi‑Fi passwords, login information);
  • Financial or payment card data encoded in certain barcode formats;
  • Health-related information if present in scanned documents;
  • Precise location data (if encoded in a barcode or QR code, e.g. geo-location URIs).

Necessity: Processing this information is necessary to provide the core scanning, decoding, and recognition features you request when you actively scan or capture a code or document.

Impact on your rights: Because scan content is stored locally on your device and not transmitted to our servers, the risk to your personal rights from our processing is limited. However, you should be aware that if you share scan results with third parties, or if device backup is enabled, the information may leave your device.

Separate consent: Processing of sensitive personal information occurs only when you actively scan or capture content. By initiating a scan, you are providing your consent for the App to decode and display the scanned content. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting the relevant scan history entries or uninstalling the App.

7. Device Backup

We retain scan history and documents on your device until you delete them in the App, clear App data, or uninstall the App.

Feedback and any contact details you submit are retained in Firestore for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, investigate issues, protect the service, and meet applicable legal obligations. 10. Security and Data Breach

We implement a range of technical and organizational measures to safeguard your data:

  • Local storage isolation: Scan history is stored in a Room database located in the App's private on-device directory, which is not accessible by other apps.
  • App preferences: User preferences are stored in DataStore within the App's private storage area.
  • Camera captures: Images captured for scanning are saved temporarily to the App's internal cache and are automatically deleted when no longer needed or when you clear the App cache from device settings.
  • Scan content: Scanning and recognition results stay on your device unless you choose to share them or include them in a feedback message. They are not automatically attached to feedback.
  • Feedback protection: Feedback uses encrypted transport, anonymous authentication, field validation, and submission limits. App clients cannot read, edit, or delete stored feedback. Access through our support administration is restricted.
  • Secure sharing: When you share files from the App, Android's FileProvider grants temporary, permission-based access only to the specific app you choose, and the permission is automatically revoked when your sharing session ends.
  • Network communication: Network requests made by the App (for example, loading the privacy policy, requesting ads and consent messages, or sending analytics and crash diagnostics) use encrypted transport such as HTTPS where supported by the service.

While we strive to protect your data, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We encourage you to take precautions such as keeping your device's operating system and apps up to date.

Data breach notification: In the event of a data breach involving your personal information, we will notify affected users without undue delay through the App, by email (if you have contacted us), or by posting a notice on this page. We will also notify relevant supervisory authorities within the timeframes required by applicable law (e.g., within 72 hours under GDPR, or per PIPL requirements for authorities in China).

11. Children's Privacy
